Concept: Archimedes screw to offload LV and propel blood to the aorta
- Pump flow a function of: 
	**Speed (rpm, in 'P-level') - set
	Flow (estimated based on speed)
	![[IMG_5172.heic]]

**Preload dependent, afterload sensitive
	Head pressure: Ao-LV (outlet - inlet, or gradient across the device)
	To maintain this, need to maintain a reasonable MAP range (60-80)
	If too high - start losing flows through the device


Impella Console
	Placement Signal: arterial pressure in the aorta (essentially aortic a-line tracing)
	Motor Current: mA of power the motor is using (also pulsatile)

In systole, head pressure goes to 0 (LV pressure has to equal aortic pressure for valve to open)
	Here, has to flow **less**
	In diastole, head pressure widens - has to flow more

![[IMG_5173.heic]]

Visualizing placement:
	Parasternal long: best view
		Inflow should **not** be blocked
		3.5cm from inflow to aortic valve in CP; 5.0cm in 5.5

Troubleshooting:
	Flat motor current - likely inflow and outflow in same place (should be pulsatile) - migrated **up** to aorta
	If migrating **down** to LV - larger difference between systole/diastole, motor current flat
**Danger** in both cases - essentially off support
	 Needs to be **replaced** if all the way out (to aorta), can be pulled back under TTE by fellow if too deep
	In either case - need to inform fellow/resident immediately

Suction events
	**Negative** pump diastolic pressures
	Occurs when inflow tract is blocked and inlet pump pressure is below LV diastolic pressure
		Causes: low LV diastolic pressures/volumes (due to hypovolemia, hemorrhage, etc)

Differential:
	LV underfilled (hypovolemia, RV failure, tamponade/tension pneumo)
	Inflow obstruction (malposition, thrombus/fibrin on inflow)
