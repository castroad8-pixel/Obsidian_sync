**Acute Tubular Necrosis**
Pathophysiology:
- Afferent arteriolar vasoconstriction in response to tubuloglomerular feedback
- Casts in urine
Etiology: 
- Severe pre-renal disease, nephrotoxins
*Less cell sloughing in non-oliguric ATN - may not present with casts!
- Phases:
	1. Initiation: hours-days
	2. Extension: ongoing hypoxia, inflammatory response (1-2 weeks)
	3. Maintenance: repairs, (1-2 weeks)
24% of patients w/ ATN require HD during hospitalization, 5% need chronic HD
Increased risk of progression to CKD4 or 5
Without CKD, risk of dialysis in next year is < 1%
