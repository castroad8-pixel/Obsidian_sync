Lymphedema vs. venous insufficiency vs. lipidmea
- **All associated with obesity***
- Venous insufficiency - classically with "upside down champagne bottle" appearance, pitting edema, dermatitis +/- ulcers
- Lymphedema - usually more uniform swelling, "tree trunk" appearance

**Fibromuscular Dysplasia
- Focal vs. multifocal, based on character of alternating stenoses/dilations (one = focal, classic 'beaded' appearance = multifocal)
- Classic symptom - pulsatile tinnitus
- Further workup - full body CTA to assess vasculature more globally
