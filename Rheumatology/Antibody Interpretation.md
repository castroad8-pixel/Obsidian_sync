**Should I repeat the ANA?
- https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa021933 (Arbuckle et al)
- **Autoantibodies typically arise before diagnosis; development of new autoantibodies follows a predictable pattern
- Conclusion: not a ton of utility in repeating autoantibody testing

***ANA: takeaways**
- Highly sensitive, nonspecific
- Also sensitive for sjogren's syndrome, scleroderma, drug-induced lupus
- High titer, more likely true positive
- Pattern - not particularly important

**Rheumatoid Factor: Pearls
- Can be false positive in setting of acute inflammation/infection
- Moderately sensitive (up to 90%) & specific (about 85%)
- Initial titer can indicate disease severity

**Anti-CCP
- Less sensitive, but **VERY SPECIFIC (95-98%)
- May correlate with more erosive/severe disease

**ANCA
- *C3PO* - proteinase-3 = C-ANCA; MPO = P-ANCA
- Both are quite specific (~98%), less sensitive (75-80%)
- 'Atypical' ANCA - no specific antigen stain or atypical P-ANCA
