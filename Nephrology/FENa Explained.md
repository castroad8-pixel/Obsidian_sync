- Classically used to distinguish prerenal from intrarenal/postrenal etiologies of AKI
- <1.0% = prerenal
**Who is this useful for?**
- Essentially people with normally functioning kidneys (i.e. no degree of CKD)
- Patients with CKD will have higher FENa as a result of decreasing nephron mass
- Low FENa = sodium-avid kidneys - of multiple etiologies (i.e. cirrhosis, east cast/obstructive nephropathy, CHD)
- High FENa = increased distal sodium delivery, 2/2 tubular damage or natriuresis
