#ambulatory 

New KDIGO guidelines:
- SGLT2i now okay for **T2DM and GFR < 20
- **Finerenone** - new non-steroidal MRA for CKD w/ max-tolerated ACE/ARB

New GOLD guidelines:
- 3 classes: A, B, E
- A: LABA or LAMA
- B: LABA + LAMA
- E: check eosinophils
	- if > 300, triple therapy inhaler (https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1713901)
	- if < 300, LABA + LAMA
- Targeting less ICS use (never use ICS without both LABA + LAMA)

GLP-1s:
- Tirzepitide interacts with oral contraceptives

New Cardiovascular risk calculator: PREVENT
(https://www.mdcalc.com/calc/10491/predicting-risk-cardiovascular-disease-events-prevent)

Menopause: new medications for vasomotor symptoms
- Most effective treatment: **still hormone therapy (estrogen or estrogen + progesterone)
	- Good if < 10 years since last menses and > 60
	- More risk if > 10 years since last menses and > 60
