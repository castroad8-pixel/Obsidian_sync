**Statin response** **& Ezetimibe**
- Goal w/ high-intensity statin: LDL < 100
- If not at goal - consider ezetimibe
- LDL target unclear for *primary prevention* 
- https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2802214
	- LODESTAR trial - "treat to target" LDL *noninferior* to high-intensity statin
	- Rosuvastatin resulted in lower achieved LDL, associated w/ more new-onset diabetes, cataracts
 - Only *one trial* of ezetimibe for secondary prevention - unproven in primary prevention
	 - ~10% incremental benefit in MACE if extrapolating to primary prevention

**Aspirin for CAD Prevention**
- USPSTF - grade *C* for ASA for primary prevention for >10% ASCVD in 40-59yo
- Recommend *against* use in >60yo
- 3 trials - all with output at similar times
- 21% higher risk of cardiovascular events for those who *stopped aspirin* after events
