**Treatments for MASLD
- Improved steatosis w/ 3-5%; NASH/fibrosis improvement requires > 10%
- Bariatric surgery: 80% resolution of NASH w/out worsening fibrosis at 1 year
- Vitamin E
	- May improve LFTs, histology, retrospective evidence fo decreased hepatic decompensation and transplant-free survival
- Pioglitazone
	- Improves histology, insulin resistance
	- May lead to NASH resolution but does not improve fibrosis
	- Risks: heart failure, bladder carcinoma, significant weight gain
- Resmetirom (approved 3/2024)
	- Thyroid hormone receptor beta (promotes hepatic fat metabolism)
	- Beta subclass is more specific liver metabolism
