DSM-V: Opioid Use Disorder
	Physiology *tolerance and withdrawal* + loss of control, continued use despite negative consequences

3 treatment options available - methadone, naltrexone, buprenorphine

Methadone: full agonist
Buprenorphine: partial agonist
Naltrexone: **antagonist** - need to be off for a long time; difficult to startwfor OUD while inpatient

Buprenorphine: partial agonist with **strong** affinity for mu receptor 
	Minimal respiratory suppression
		Ceiling effect: doses > 24mg do not significantly decrease respiratory drive
	Worry: precipitated withdrawal
		**Blocks** other opioids, **displaces** other opioids - **not** life-threatening

MOUD: fewer overdoses

Methadone - more likely to stay in treatment, fewer infectious complications, improved birth outcomes
